How the data pipeline works

  1. Discovery. For each topic, the series query runs and the documented selection rule is applied (see each topic page). The series are ranked by volume_fp and the top 30 are kept.
  2. Universe. The open markets of those series are fetched with GET /markets?series_ticker=…&status=open, and each market is tagged with its topic and with the series' official settlement sources.
  3. Refresh. Every few seconds the tracked tickers are re-read in batches of 40 with GET /markets?tickers=…. Each new observation is compared with the previous one.
  4. Alerts. A move of at least the threshold (default $0.10) posts a message to the chat panes, with a 60-second cooldown per ticker and direction. A status change to determined or finalized with a result of yes or no posts a resolution message.
  5. Fallback. If the API cannot be reached, the committed snapshot is loaded instead and the site says so in the header badge and the notice at the top of every page.

Fixed-point values

Counts and prices arrive as fixed-point strings — volume_fp, open_interest_fp, yes_bid_size_fp and the *_dollars fields. The site converts them to numbers for display and shows four decimal places for prices, matching the precision the exchange publishes. Reference: Fixed Point Migration.
