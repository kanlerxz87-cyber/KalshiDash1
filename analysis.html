Trade categories used by this page

A paper trade is categorised twice. These are project tags, not exchange recommendations:

  • Strategy — which rule fired (momentum, mean reversion, favorite, longshot, volume catalyst, or manual).
  • Topic — the focus topic of the series the market belongs to, taken from the series query that discovered the market.

The exact rule for each strategy, including its thresholds, is published on Strategies, so a result can be reproduced rather than taken on trust.

Trade-type categories come from the simulated order record itself; the market's topic comes from GET /markets?series_ticker=…&status=open filtered by the series queries on API reference.
