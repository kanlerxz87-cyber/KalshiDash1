Market states used by this tracker

The REST API returns these lifecycle statuses for a market, and result becomes yes, no or scalar once the outcome is known:

initialized — created, not yet trading active — open for trading inactive — paused by the exchange closed — past close time, awaiting determination determined — result known, settlement timer running disputed — result challenged amended — re-determined after a dispute finalized — settled, positions paid

Source: Market Lifecycle. On a YES outcome the YES holders receive $1.00 per contract; on a NO outcome the NO holders receive $1.00 per contract — Market Settlement.
