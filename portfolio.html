How the settlement rule is applied

  • On a yes outcome the YES holders receive $1.00 per contract; on a no outcome the NO holders receive $1.00 per contract — Market Settlement.
  • A paper trade is settled when the market reaches determined or finalized with a result of yes or no — Market Lifecycle.
  • Realised P&L = (payout − entry price) × contracts. Fees are not applied: Kalshi states settlement fees are zero for simple yes/no determinations, and the simulator does not model a trading fee.
  • Mark-to-market uses the last observed price, not a guaranteed exit price.

Trades are stored in this browser's local storage only. Clearing the browser data removes them.
