Review-before-implement workflow

  1. A suggestion is proposed (status: Proposed).
  2. It is listed here with an owner, impact estimate, and required verification.
  3. A reviewer marks it Under Review, checks it against official sources, and either approves or rejects.
  4. Approved items are implemented; rejected items are closed with a reason.
  5. No change is implemented without a recorded review decision.
