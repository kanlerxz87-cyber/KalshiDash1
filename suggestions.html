Suggestions for Better Decision Making

This section is dedicated to suggestions for improving the site and your trading decisions. Review each suggestion and choose the best path before implementing any changes. None of these are applied automatically.

#SuggestionWhy it helps decision makingEffortStatus
1 Add Kalshi WebSocket subscription (wss://external-api-ws.demo.kalshi.co/trade-api/ws/v2) for real-time price ticks instead of 3-second polling. Faster, push-based price movement on the graph with no stale polls. Medium Proposed
2 Store Kalshi demo API key securely (env/secret, never in the repo) and call GET /portfolio, GET /trades, POST /portfolio/orders for live execution. Enables real demo-account performance tracking and actual trade execution rather than paper simulation. High Proposed
3 Add historical price candles endpoint (/markets/{ticker}/candles) to backfill the graph so the X/Y chart starts with real history, not just live ticks. Lets you analyze price trends over seconds/minutes/hours from the moment a market is selected. Medium Proposed

5 Add "highest dollar amount" ranking using notional volume (price × size × contracts) rather than open interest alone. Surfaces the truly largest-dollar live events first. Low Proposed
6 Add resolved-trade export (CSV) from the paper tracker so results can be analyzed offline by trade type. Supports strategy creation based on the type of trade. Low Proposed
7 Add settlement-source verification per market (display the event's settlement sources from /events). Lets you confirm how each event will be resolved before trading. Low Proposed
8 Add price alerts (e.g. notify when Yes bid crosses a threshold) for watched markets. Helps act on price movements without watching the graph constantly. Medium Proposed
