Questions & Answers — Pre-Decision Review

Clear list of questions, information, and answers that must be addressed before any decision can reasonably be made. Items we do not have are flagged as missing, with suggestions to discover them.

How to use

Every decision based on this dashboard should wait until the questions below are answered. "Missing" means the information is not available from an official source or was not provided to this session.

Question register

Missing-information discovery plan

  • Demo account: follow the Kalshi demo account tutorial.
  • API key: generate one in the demo environment (docs); set window.KALSHI_API_KEY in DevTools and refresh.
  • Live data: open the browser console and inspect window.KalshiDash.STATE after connecting.
  • All unverified values: see Flagged for Review.
