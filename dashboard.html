Orderbook liquidity — contracts available at each price

Each level shows a price and how many contracts are available (bid size) on the YES and NO sides. In Kalshi binary markets, a YES bid at price X equals a NO ask at (100 − X); only bids are returned by the public orderbook endpoint.

How to use

  1. Open a demo account at demo.kalshi.co and find a market ticker (e.g. from the demo site or GET /markets?status=open).
  2. Enter the market ticker above (optionally the series ticker) and click Load market data.
  3. Watch the live price graph and liquidity table. Change the graph-length selector to view seconds / minutes / hours of price history.
  4. Prices and liquidity come straight from the Kalshi demo API in your browser; they may lag the exchange by a few seconds (data is cached briefly).
