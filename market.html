A YES ask is 1 − NO bid, which is the relation the exchange documents for a binary book. Both executable sides are shown because a one-sided book (a bid with no offer, or an offer with no bid) means the headline price cannot be traded — that is exactly the condition the high-ROI model now refuses to score.
