A YES ask is 1 − NO bid, which is the relation the exchange documents for a binary book. Each derived ask shares the quantity of its complementary bid. A one-sided book may expose only one direction: do not assume missing opposite-side depth. A point-in-time book is not a guaranteed fill. Official book mechanics.

Simulated trade

This records a paper trade in your browser against the live price. It never sends an order to Kalshi. It requires a fresh live ask and enough observed contracts; snapshot data cannot create a fill. The Demo account page is a separate, read-only account snapshot; signing keys never enter this browser.
