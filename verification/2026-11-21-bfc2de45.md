- `data/live/feed.json` contains **99** alert records. All 99 are for the same ticker, `KXPRESNOMD-28-AKLO`, from 2026-11-20 16:28:00Z through 19:25:54Z (90 trade detections and 9 candle detections). The largest recorded move is `$0.80`; 18 rows have `model.highROI: true`.
- The same feed's `run` object says the 2026-11-21 00:55:11Z sample created **0** alerts and **0** resolutions. The feed metadata says a clean restart/reset occurred, while [docs/ALERTS.md](../docs/ALERTS.md) says “0 after the reset” and that the feeds say so. The committed `alerts` array therefore conflicts with the current narrative/reset description.
- The current official market endpoint reports a `$0.0030` last price and `$0.0020`–`$0.0030` YES book. This later market state does not, by itself, prove what historical prices were at the alert timestamps. The historical `$0.80` swing should not be acted on until its source/timestamp integrity is reviewed.
- **Do not treat the 99 rows as a count of 99 distinct market events** or as validated ROI performance. An open PR, [#39](https://github.com/kanlerxz87-cyber/KalshiDash1/pull/39), addresses repeated same-ticker alert flooding, but this audit did not change or merge that code.
- Sources: [committed feed](../data/live/feed.json) (includes exact API `source_url` per record), [official current market response](https://external-api.demo.kalshi.co/trade-api/v2/markets/KXPRESNOMD-28-AKLO), [official trades endpoint](https://external-api.demo.kalshi.co/trade-api/v2/markets/trades?ticker=KXPRESNOMD-28-AKLO&min_ts=1795183734&limit=10), and [alert report](../docs/ALERTS.md).
