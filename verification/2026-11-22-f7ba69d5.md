## 3. F-86 — the defect, measured

**The defect.** `corroborate()` in `tools/live_engine.mjs` accepted `previous_price_dollars` as a second published
field that could prove a sampled swing was real, and its code comment described the field as "the price of the trade
before the last one". S1 shows it is the last traded YES price **a day ago**. A day-old value is not a same-session
observation: on a market that moved during the day it can agree with a print that the live book contradicts.
`assets/catalyst.js` had the same wrong idea and published the day-over-day difference as a swing with basis
`prev-close`, i.e. an intraday detection.

**Measured scope on the committed sample (`data/live/feed.json`, `generated_at 2026-11-22T02:27:14.000Z`).** Re-check
with: `node --input-type=module -e "import {corroborate} from './tools/live_engine.mjs'; …"` (one-liner recorded in §5).

- 40 alerts total.
- **3** alerts recorded `corroboration.by = "previous_price_dollars"`
  (`KXBTCY-27JAN0100-B87500@1795303493`, `KXPRESPERSON-28-VRAM@1795303106`, `KXBTCY-27JAN0100-B77500@1795302737`).
- Re-checked against their own recorded book, **all 3** are also corroborated by the live book
  (`yes_bid`/`yes_ask` 0.01/0.02, 0.002/0.003 and 0.03/0.04 respectively).
- **0** alerts in this sample were admitted by the day-old field alone.

**Therefore the honest finding is:** a false provenance claim plus a latent acceptance hole — **not** a demonstrated
false alert in this sample. The stronger claim ("a day-old price let a fake swing through here") is **not** made,
because the data does not support it. The defect is real independently of that: the field cannot be same-session
evidence by definition, and the gate now rejects it structurally.

**Fix (files).**
- `tools/live_engine.mjs` — `corroborate()` reads only the same-session book (YES quotes, or 1 − NO quotes). The
  docstring now quotes the official definition and records the measured case. Trade- and candle-detected alerts are
  unchanged: their price *is* a published trade.
- `assets/catalyst.js` — `detectPrevCloseSwing` → `detect24hChange`, basis `prev-close` → `24h-change`, documented as a
  day-over-day comparison that is never folded into the ≥ $0.10 intraday feed.
- `templates/catalyst.tpl` — methodology and source row S2 corrected to the official field definitions.
- `scripts/check_alerts.mjs` — mechanical guard: an alert may not cite `previous_price_dollars` as corroboration.
  Rows published before the fix keep their recorded evidence and must be listed in
  `meta.legacy_day_old_corroboration`, which records `book_also_corroborates` per row.
- `scripts/annotate-legacy-day-old-corroboration.mjs` (new) — idempotent; changes only `meta`; verified byte-identical
  on re-run, and `git diff --numstat` shows 34 insertions / 1 deletion per data file (the `meta` block only).
- `scripts/check_lab.mjs` — the lab assertion now requires `24h-change` and forbids the old `prev-close` label.

## 5. F-86 — live-signal UX

Two columns added to the alert console's per-topic board, using only published fields:

- **Board** — marks the top-ranked row of each topic on the board's existing ranking (estimated traded dollars), with
  every tied row marked. A row with no estimate is never crowned.
- **24h change** — `last_price_dollars` vs `previous_price_dollars` (S1), printed with an explicit sign; blank (`—`)
  when either field is missing. This makes a contract that oscillates between two extremes visible *as oscillating*
  next to the alert chats, instead of each print looking like a fresh opportunity.
