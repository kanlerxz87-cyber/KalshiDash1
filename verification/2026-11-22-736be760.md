### 2.2 Order book / liquidity semantics

Source: [Orderbook Responses](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/orderbook_responses), fetched 2026-11-22.

- *"The orderbook response is wrapped in an `orderbook_fp` object containing two arrays of bids: `yes_dollars` for YES
  positions and `no_dollars` for NO positions."* → only bids exist in REST.
- *"Arrays are sorted by price in **ascending order** … The **highest** bid (best bid) is the **last** element."*
- *"Kalshi's orderbook only returns bids, not asks"*; *"A **YES BID** at price X is equivalent to a **NO ASK** at price
  ($1.00 - X)"*; best YES ask = `$1.00 - highest NO bid`.
- *"A market with no resting orders returns empty `yes_dollars` and `no_dollars` arrays. So does a ticker that does not
  match any market: the endpoint returns `200` with empty arrays rather than `404`."*

Cross-check against the committed engine output: `data/live/feed.json` → `orderbooks["KXPRESNOMD-28-BOBA"]` has
`best_no_bid: 0.071` and `implied_yes_ask: 0.929`, i.e. `1 − 0.071 = 0.929`. The code's derivation matches the documented
reciprocity, and the ladder keeps API bids separate from implied asks. **Confirmed.**

### 2.3 Candlesticks (the chart's history source)

Source: [Get Market Candlesticks](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-market-candlesticks), fetched 2026-11-22.

- Path: *`/series/{series_ticker}/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks`*.
- *"`period_interval` … Time period length of each candlestick in minutes. Valid values are 1 (1 minute), 60 (1 hour), or
  1440 (1 day)."*
- OHLC fields are `*_dollars` strings plus `volume_fp` and `open_interest_fp` per candlestick.

The site's chart therefore cannot offer true seconds history from REST; it uses 1/60/1440-minute periods and local poll
observations for seconds. That limitation is stated on the site (executive summary §4) — **consistent with the docs.**

### 2.4 Settlement and `result` (the yes/no tracking requirement)

Sources: [Market Settlement](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/market_settlement) and
[Settlement Bounds](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/settlement_bounds), both fetched 2026-11-22.

- *"**Yes outcome**: Yes contract holders receive $1 per contract"* · *"**No outcome**: No contract holders receive $1 per
  contract"* · *"Only net positions are settled (after netting)"* · *"Settlement fees are zero for simple yes/no
  determinations but may apply for sub-cent **scalar** settlement."*
- Settlement Bounds: *"Certain markets have a **scalar payout** … A government shutdown market settles at $0.10 for each
  day the shutdown lasts … The market settles at $0.50 … YES holders receive the value the market settles at minus the
  floor, per contract. NO holders receive $1 minus the value the market settles at."*

This is the source that decides finding **F-84** in §4: the $1.00 rule is a rule about the two binary outcomes only.

## 4. Defect found and fixed — F-84: scalar payouts were published as "$1.00 per contract"

**Published claim (before this session).** `templates/executive-summary.tpl`, rendered at
[`executive-summary.html`](../executive-summary.html) line 121:

> Markets resolve with `yes`, `no` or `scalar`; the winning side is paid $1.00 per contract.

**Why it is wrong.** The sentence attaches the $1.00 payout to all three `result` values. §2.4 shows the official pages
support $1 per contract only for the *yes* and *no* binary outcomes, and describe scalar markets settling at a value
(`$0.50` in the worked example) with a floor, and note *"sub-cent scalar settlement"*. A scalar market therefore does not
pay a flat $1.00, and a reader could have priced a scalar contract as a certain win.

**Fix (this session).** The sentence now gives the $1 rule only for a simple binary contract's yes/no outcomes, states that
a scalar market pays the value it settles at (minus any settlement floor for YES), and says the site never prices a scalar
contract as a binary win/loss — with links to both official pages and to F-84.

**Scope check before editing.** The other five published places that mention $1.00 were re-read at the same time:
`templates/index.tpl:65` ("the winning *binary* side"), `markets.tpl:73`, `portfolio.tpl:67–68`, `catalyst.tpl:87`
("scalar results follow different payout bounds"), `questions.tpl:46` ("Scalar payouts follow their own settlement bounds
and are not modeled"). All five were already conditional on a yes/no outcome and were left untouched.

**Rebuild and gates after the fix:** (finding ID is **F-84**, renumbered from F-82 see §5 overlap) `node scripts/build-site.mjs` regenerated 22 pages (`executive-summary.html`,
`flagged.html` changed; navigation and every other page byte-identical); `npm test`, `build-site --check`,
`check-links.mjs` and `linkcheck.py` were re-run and pass (§6).

**Published on the Flagged page** as **F-84** with both official sources, the corrected sentence, the template and this
record.
