## Reproducible observations

- `alerts/index.html` described the feed as showing every qualifying move and scrolling "in real time". Engine-only mode in `alerts/js/alerts-page.js` said the scheduled feed "stays current". Live mode described new swings as appearing "within seconds" although the browser polls on a 30-second timer. These claims exceeded the implementation and observed cadence.
- At `2026-11-27T22:25:09Z`, `gh run list --workflow live-alerts.yml --limit 50` showed the most recent schedule-triggered live-alert run at `2026-11-27T14:24:37Z` (run [46015672033](https://github.com/kanlerxz87-cyber/KalshiDash1/actions/runs/46015672033)); preceding schedule runs shown were at `04:43:59Z`, `2026-11-26T22:56:58Z`, and `2026-11-26T14:09:00Z`. Those observed gaps do not meet the declared `*/10` cron. This is consistent with existing [Flagged for review F-83](https://kanlerxz87-cyber.github.io/KalshiDash1/flagged.html#F-83) and GitHub's documented best-effort schedule behavior; it is not evidence that any particular missed run had a specific cause.
- The committed feed's `meta.generated_at` / `run.sampled_at` was `2026-11-27T17:34:38Z`; at the CLI observation time above it was 4h 50m old. The pages already display the measured sample age; no age or market price was altered in this task.
- Public documentation identifies authenticated WebSockets, so the static site does not claim a keyless WebSocket stream. Browser REST polling remains best-effort and may not capture intervening moves.
