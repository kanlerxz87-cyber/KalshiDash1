## 3. Measured conclusions (and what they do not prove)

1. **A keyless static page cannot stream demo ticks.** Measured, both documented demo hosts, 2026-11-27: no anonymous upgrade is answered affirmatively (recommended host: silence; legacy host: 301→401). The published limitation (F-02/F-33/F-51) now has transport evidence, not only documentation.

3. **Browser reads of public REST endpoints remain permitted** (fresh CORS evidence, same verdict as 2026-11-21).
4. **Not proven (and not attempted):** that an *authenticated* WS handshake works — the tool holds no credentials by design; nothing about production hosts (guard refuses them); nothing about a specific visitor's network (ISP/proxy blocking is per-network; the site already reports fallbacks at runtime).
