## 5. Irregularities found today (candidates for the Flagged for Review page)

The Flagged page and `EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md` are owned by other sessions, so these carry this session's own local ids
(V-n). They must be renumbered into the F-series by whoever owns that file; the highest F-id on `main` today is **F-83**.

**V-1 — the published feed is stale and the scheduled sampler has stopped.** `node tools/freshness_check.mjs` fails:
the feed's `meta.generated_at` is `2026-11-27T14:24:49.000Z`; measured at `2026-11-27T17:22:08Z` that is **177.3 min**,
against a 45-minute limit. `gh run list --workflow live-alerts.yml` shows the last scheduled run `46015672033`
succeeded at 14:24:37Z and **no scheduled run since**. Per S7 this is the documented delay/drop behaviour of scheduled
workflows. Consequence: every number on the live site is ~3 h old, and the freshness badge is correctly reporting the
staleness rather than hiding it. *Blank, not inferred:* what the price is now.

**V-2 — "highest dollar amount" is this repository's estimate, not an exchange figure.** `estimateTradedDollars()` is
`round(volume_fp × price, 2)` (`tools/live_engine.mjs:309`); Kalshi's market object publishes contract volume but no
traded-dollar field (S3). The engine labels it `est_traded_dollars` and the pages carry that wording. *Consequence for
the user's question:* the ranking is "highest estimated dollars traded", reproducible from two published fields, not
"highest dollars" as a Kalshi-published number. Nothing is blank here — the estimate is shown openly — but it must not
be read as exchange-reported turnover.

**V-3 — every published high-ROI row is a previous-sample jump with no trade print or candle behind it, and all of
them are large demo discontinuities.** Measured on the committed feed: 121 alerts; **20** carry `model.highROI:true`,
all 20 `tradeable:true`, `abs_delta` between **0.765 and 0.985**, topics elections 13 / crypto 6 / gas 1. All 20 have
`detection` absent, `trade:null`, `candle:null`, `baseline_kind:"previous sample"`, and their only published support is
a book-midpoint `corroboration`. Across the whole feed, **121/121 alerts have `abs_delta` ≥ 0.50** and **111/121 sit at
≥ $0.95 or ≤ $0.05** — e.g. 75 crypto alerts on `KXBTCY-*` flipping `$0.01↔$0.97` (32 in that one direction), and
`KXPRESPERSON-28-GABB` reading `$0.815 → $0.003`. This is the pattern S1 warns about (demo prices need not reflect real
markets) and the pattern the engine's own comment at `:370` already records for `KXPRESNOMD-28-AKLO`. *Blank, not
inferred:* no claim is made that any of these represent a real market move. The high-ROI chat must therefore be read as
"the model liked these", not "these were tradeable in reality".

**V-4 — no alert in the current sample exercises the $0.10 boundary.** Threshold discipline is enforced in code and by
`scripts/check_alerts.mjs:115`, but the published snapshot's smallest `abs_delta` is 0.50, so a reader of the live site
today sees only extreme swings. Not a defect; a sampling limitation worth stating.

**V-5 — the demo account is not connected, so no portfolio number can be verified.** `data/account.json`:
`connected:false`, `captured_at:null`. There is no balance, position, fill or P&L to check against an exchange response,
so R7/R8 are reported as *unverified* rather than filled with a plausible figure. Prerequisites are documented in
`docs/DEMO_ACCOUNT.md` and `demo-account.html`.

**V-6 — 21 of 121 alerts carry no detection provenance.** These rows have neither `detection`, a `trade` block nor a
`candle` block (`trade:null`, `candle:null`), so a reader cannot tell from the feed whether the swing came from a trade
