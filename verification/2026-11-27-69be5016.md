## 2 · The defect, measured

The requirement is a live, interactive price chart whose X axis is time with selectable **seconds /
minutes / hours** windows and whose Y axis is the contract price. The alert console
(`alerts/index.html`) implements that chart in `alerts/js/alerts-page.js` `drawChart()`.

`drawChart()` obtained its points from `A.history(ticker)`, and `history()` returns either
`state.live.history` (samples this tab recorded while open) or `feed.history` (one point per engine
run). The renderer refuses to draw a line with fewer than two points (`pts.length < 2`).

Measurement over the committed feed `data/live/feed.json` (`meta.generated_at`
`2026-11-27T17:34:38Z`), over the same 25 candidates the chart's own picker ranks highest by
`est_traded_dollars`:

| Window offered | Candidates with ≥ 2 points |
|---|---|
| 5 min | **0 / 25** |
| 1 hour | **0 / 25** |
| 6 hours | 4 / 25 |
| 24 hours | 4 / 25 |
| 7 days | 4 / 25 |

**21 of the 25 candidates had no `history` array at all.** So two of the five advertised windows
could never draw a line from the committed record, and four of five drew it for only a sixth of
their candidates.

Why the feed is so sparse here is already documented, not re-litigated: the engine declares
`cron: '*/10 * * * *'` but GitHub delivers scheduled runs far less often (**F-83**). Measured on
2026-11-27, `live-alerts` schedule-triggered runs occurred at 14:24Z and then not again within the
recorded window — a ~10 h gap, i.e. roughly one point per ten hours rather than one per ten minutes.

Live URL for the same page, for manual review:
<https://kanlerxz87-cyber.github.io/KalshiDash1/alerts/index.html>

## 4 · The fix

`alerts/js/alerts-live.js`

* `candleIntervalFor(windowSec)` picks the documented interval that keeps the request inside the
  5,000-period cap: 1 for every window up to 24 h (`86,400 / 60 = 1,440` periods), 60 for 7 days
  (`10,080 / 60 = 168` periods).
* `fetchCandles(ticker, windowSec, {force})` calls the official path with integer second timestamps,
  caches per market for 60 s, and never rejects: a failure is stored as `slot.error`.
* Mapping to points follows the official schema exactly: `end_period_ts` → `t`,
  `price.close_dollars` → `p`. A **null** close is dropped, never filled; a value outside `$0–$1` is
  rejected. The cache entry keeps `source_url` (the exact request) and `basis` ("official 1-minute
  traded close").
* `chartSeries(ticker, windowSec, nowSec)` is the single merge used by the page: official traded
  closes first, then `history()` samples, de-duplicated by timestamp with the **official print
  winning** (it is the traded price; a REST sample is a book price), ordered by time, and clipped to
  the window. Exported so the offline gate can assert it without a canvas.

`alerts/js/alerts-page.js`

* `drawChart()` kicks off `A.fetchCandles(...)` and redraws on completion; `drawChartPoints()` does
  the drawing, so a slow or blocked request can never wipe the chart.
* Windows are now 60 s · 5 min · 15 min · 1 h · 6 h · 24 h · 7 d, each with a tooltip naming the
  official period that backs it. Note the honest limit: the exchange's finest documented period is
  1 minute, so a 60-second window is drawn from the 1-minute official close plus this tab's own
  samples — it cannot be finer than the publisher allows.
* The caption now reads e.g. `KXBTCY-… · 42 point(s) (12 official traded closes, 1-min candle) ·
  window 1 h`, and appends `· candlestick request failed: …` when the official call failed.
* The empty state names the reason (no market selected / request failed / exchange published no
  traded close in this window / still in flight) instead of implying the contract did not move.

`alerts/index.html`

* Legend and chart heading updated; a paragraph under the chart links the official endpoint
  documentation and states that a no-trade period is skipped, never interpolated, and cites
  `F-CHART-02`.

Nothing in this fix reads, writes or re-derives a market value that the exchange did not publish.
