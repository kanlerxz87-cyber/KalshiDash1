## 4. F-94 — the selective pane's composition (this session's finding)

Reproduce: `node scripts/highroi-composition.mjs`.

| Measurement | Result |
|---|---|
| `highROI: true` rows | **20 of 121** (16.5%) |
| Direction | **up 0 / down 20** |
| Premised within $0.05 of an extreme ($0.97–$0.99) | **10 of 20** |
| Size term saturated (`|Δ|` ≥ $0.50) | **20 of 20** |
| Liquidity term saturated (24h ≥ 1,000 contracts) | **20 of 20** |
| Score range | 0.779 – 0.8767 (median 0.8199) |
| Topics / tickers | elections 13, crypto 6, gas 1 / 15 distinct tickers |

**The arithmetic, from the published weights** (`docs/ALERTS.md` §3.4, `alerts/index.html` method table):
with size = 1.00 and liquidity = 1.00 the score is `0.775 + 0.20·headroom + 0.25·(spread−0.90)`. A row that
started at $0.99 with a penny-wide book therefore scores **0.775 before any headroom is counted**, above the
0.62 threshold. Headroom at $0.99 is 0.02, worth at most 0.005. Raising the threshold to 0.75 leaves the same
20 rows.

**Withdrawn remedy, disclosed.** This session first implemented a `$0.05` headroom floor and wrote on the board
that it capped the base score at 0.575. Its own selftest case failed and showed the correct value is **0.775**
(the earlier figure silently subtracted the saturated size and liquidity weights). The change was reverted with
`git checkout tools/live_engine.mjs` **before publication**; `node tools/live_engine.mjs --selftest` passes on the
restored file; **no score, row or verdict was changed**. A new weight without a back-test would be a guess, which
this project does not publish, so the defect is published and gated instead:

- `scripts/highroi-composition.mjs` — reproducible report + invariant gate (every `highROI` row must meet
  `meta.high_roi_threshold` and be `tradeable`); `--check` exits non-zero on a violation.
- Wired into `npm test` (`test:highroi`) and the `site-qa` workflow.
- Published as **F-94** (needs review) with the composition and arithmetic, and cross-referenced from
  `docs/ALERTS.md` and the console's method table.
