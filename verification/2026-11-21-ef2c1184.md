## 4. Defect found and fixed (F-66)

Reproduced on the committed engine before any edit (in-process, no network):

```
# 401-row sample, then a later settled result for a market that fell out of the 300-row cap
markets kept: 300   SMALL kept? false
resolution recorded for pruned SMALL? 0   untracked_settled_seen: 1
```

Root causes, both measured:

1. **Retention.** `applySample()` capped `next.markets` at 300 rows ranked by
   `est_traded_dollars`, then treated anything not in that map as “untracked”, discarding the
   result. The 2026-11-21 17:42:16Z feed shows the consequence: `resolutions: []` with
   `run.untracked_settled_seen: 500`.
2. **Retrieval.** `collectResolutions()` requested one page of
   `?series_ticker=…&status=settled&limit=100`. Per S1, `status=settled` alone is ordered by
   **creation** time, newest first, so recent settlements are not on the first page; the
   settlement-time ordering requires `min_settled_ts`/`max_settled_ts`, and `limit` may be 1000.
   It also re-polled `[...trackedSeries].slice(0, 12)` — insertion order, not need.

Fix (all in `tools/live_engine.mjs`, plus the checks and views listed in §5):

- pending markets only (`not in resolutions`), series ordered by most recent `close_time`;
- `status=settled&min_settled_ts=<previous sample − 900 s>&limit=1000`, cursor followed for at
  most 2 pages;
- alerted tickers never evicted while unanswered (and for 7 days after), `settled_markets`
  remembers tracked tickers, resolved rows keep `result`/`status`/`settlement_ts`/`settlement_value_dollars`;
- `awaitingResolutions()` publishes the follow list; `scripts/backfill-awaiting.mjs` derives it
  from the committed `state.json` (single writer; `--check` fails when stale).

Selftest assertion count after the change: **72** (48 before; 14 new, covering retention,
re-creation of the resolved row, the untracked counter, paging past page 1, the pending-only
filter, close-time ordering, `min_settled_ts` derivation and `limit=1000`).
