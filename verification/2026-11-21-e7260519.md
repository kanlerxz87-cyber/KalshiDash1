## 1. What was measured, and how

### 1.1 The published high-ROI pane ranked swings that could not be executed

Read directly from the committed `data/live/feed.json` (`meta.generated_at` `2026-11-21T17:42:16.000Z`):
38 alert rows, 6 with `model.highROI: true`. Comparing each high-ROI row's `direction`/`to` with the
`yes_bid_dollars`/`yes_ask_dollars` published **in the same row**:

| # | alert id | published move | YES bid | YES ask | executable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `trade:KXBTCY-27JAN0100-B97500@1795282837` | $0.01 → $0.97 up | 0.0000 | **0.0100** | no — nothing to buy at $0.97 |
| 2 | `trade:KXBTCY-27JAN0100-B97500@1795281934` | $0.01 → $0.97 up | 0.0000 | **0.0100** | no |
| 3 | `trade:KXBTCY-27JAN0100-B97500@1795280960` | $0.97 → $0.01 down | **0.0000** | 0.0100 | no — no bid to sell into |
| 4 | `trade:KXBTCY-27JAN0100-B97500@1795280058` | $0.97 → $0.01 down | **0.0000** | 0.0100 | no |
| 5 | `trade:KXPRESNOMD-28-AKLO@1795200544` | $0.803 → $0.003 down | **0.0000** | 0.8030 | no — no bid to sell into |
| 6 | `trade:KXBTCY-27JAN0100-B62500@…` | $0.72 → $0.18 down | 0.1700 | 0.1800 | yes (bid within $0.01) |

So **5 of 6** top-ranked rows were unfillable. The mechanism was in `modelScore`:

* the spread term computed `ask − bid` and required only `ask > 0`, so a **one-sided book**
  (bid `0.0000`, ask `0.9700`) scored `1 − 0.97 × 10 = 0.90` — the model treated "no buyers at all"
  as "a tight, cheap book";
* nothing compared the post-swing quote with the swing price at all.

Reproduced as a negative check (`/tmp/old_model_check.mjs`, formula copied verbatim from the pre-fix file):
the old code returns `score 0.779, highROI true` for row 1 and `score 0.6288, highROI true` for row 5 —
**the exact scores that were published**. The new assertions therefore demonstrably can fail.

### 1.2 Why those prints exist at all (official endpoints, fetched 2026-11-21 ~19:34 Z / 20:4x Z)

All through the official demo host, from this sandbox using the platform fetch tool (`curl` is blocked by the
sandbox egress policy — same limitation recorded as R-02 by an earlier session):

* `GET https://demo-api.kalshi.co/trade-api/v2/markets/trades?ticker=KXBTCY-27JAN0100-B97500&limit=8`
  → inside **one second** (19:34:10–19:34:20Z) individual 0.24–1.85 contract trades printed
  `yes_price_dollars` `0.0100`, then `0.9700`, with `no_price_dollars` `0.9900` ↔ `0.0300`.
* `GET …/markets/KXBTCY-27JAN0100-B97500/orderbook` →
  `{"orderbook_fp":{"no_dollars":[["0.0300","2991498.81"],["0.9700","5555.55"],…],"yes_dollars":[]}}`
  i.e. an **empty YES side** and real NO size, which by the documented `1 − NO bid` relation is a
  $0.97 YES ask.
* `GET …/markets/KXBTCY-27JAN0100-B97500` → `last_price_dollars 0.9700`, `previous_price_dollars 0.9700`,
  `yes_bid_dollars 0.0000`, `yes_ask_dollars 0.9700`, `volume_24h_fp 39573.86`.
* `GET …/exchange/status` → `exchange_active true`, `trading_active true`.

Conclusion drawn: the swings are real recorded official data, but at the recorded price they were not
tradeable. The defect was in how this project scored them, not in the exchange response. Nothing about the
exchange is claimed to be broken.

### Two defects this session found in its *own* work, and fixed rather than shipped

1. **A test that hung.** The new 30 s age tick kept jsdom alive, so `check_freshness_badge.mjs` never exited
   (measured: 120 s, exit 124). Fixed in the checker (each case closes its jsdom window) and in the script

   (observed: "Committed site is stale for: market.html"). The age is now browser-computed; the committed
   HTML carries the absolute timestamp and the project's own rule, so `--check` is deterministic again.
