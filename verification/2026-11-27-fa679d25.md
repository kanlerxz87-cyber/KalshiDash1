## 2. Reproducible Defects Found & Fixed

### `F-ALERTS-01` — Finalized/settled contracts contaminating live boards & polling budgets
- **Observed on `d6033eb` (`data/live/feed.json`, `2026-11-27T22:26:30Z`):**
  - `feed.by_topic.golf` contained **13 of 24 finalized contracts**, crowning finalized Nov 23 loser `KXPGATOUR-THRC26-BHAR` as `#1 leader` in `alerts/index.html` under "Highest-dollar live events, kept separate by topic", followed by finalized `#2` `KXPGATOUR-THRC26-MPEN`.
  - `feed.by_topic.gas` contained **3 of 17 finalized contracts** (`KXAAAGASW-26NOV23-3.6200`, `-3.6000`, `-3.6400`).
  - `topMarketsByTopic()` also fed trade-swing polling, candlestick-swing polling, and `state.history` retention, and `collectOrderbooks()` had no status filter — wasting live API budgets on settled contracts.
- **Root cause:** `boardByTopic()`, `topMarkets()`, `topMarketsByTopic()`, and `collectOrderbooks()` in `tools/live_engine.mjs` lacked a live-lifecycle predicate, and `topicRows()` in `alerts/js/alerts-page.js` rendered `feed.by_topic` without filtering out finalized/resolved rows.
- **Fix:**
  - Added exported `isLiveMarket(m)` in `tools/live_engine.mjs` (`!m.result` and `status` in `['', 'active', 'initialized']`) and applied it to `boardByTopic()`, `topMarkets()`, `topMarketsByTopic()`, `collectOrderbooks()`, and `slimHistory()` (indirectly via `topMarkets`).
  - In `applySample()`, added `pendingSettlement` retention so closed/determined/disputed/amended non-finalized markets are still kept in `state.markets` for `collectDirectResolutions()` while excluded from live boards.
  - Added matching `isLiveRow(m)` in `alerts/js/alerts-page.js` (`topicRows()`, `renderMarkets()`, `chartCandidates()`), so even a pre-refresh `feed.json` immediately renders only active open contracts on the live site.
- **Measured on real committed `data/live/state.json` after fix:**
  - `boardByTopic.golf`: 24/24 active (`nonLive=0`), `#1 = KXDPWORLDTOUR-BMAPC26-HBAR (status=active)`
  - `boardByTopic.gas`: 14/14 active (`nonLive=0`), `#1 = KXAAAGASM-26NOV30-3.90 (status=active)`
  - `topMarkets`: 60/60 active (`nonLive=0`); `topMarketsByTopic`: 16/16 active (`nonLive=0`).
