## 2. Verified Defects Reproduced and Fixed

### `F-GAS-01` — EIA Natural Gas series leaked into Gas Prices, contradicting AAA rule & F-05 and displacing an active AAA series
- **Measured on published `categories/gas.html` (`main` @ `44a47d0`/`8838a4a`):**
  - Line 30 (`Selection rule`) stated: *"Keep a series when its ticker starts with 'KXAAAGAS' ... or its title contains 'gas price'. Verified against GET /series?category=Economics&tags=Oil and energy — every kept series settles against AAA (gasprices.aaa.com)."*
  - `flagged.html#F-05` explicitly stated: *"Oil & Gas tag includes WTI and natural gas series, which are excluded."*
  - Yet `data/snapshot.json` (`topics.gas.series`, 30 rows) and `categories/gas.html` contained **two EIA natural gas futures series** matched by the unanchored `/gas price/i` predicate on `"Natural gas price peak"`:
    - `KXNGASMAX` — rank **#5** (volume 42,841, `categories/gas.html` line 75), settlement source **Energy Information Administration** (`https://www.eia.gov/dnav/ng/NG_PRI_FUT_S1_D.htm`).
    - `NGASMAX` — rank **#14** (volume 3,799, `categories/gas.html` line 156), settlement source **Energy Information Administration**.
  - The remaining 28 of 30 series were `KXAAAGAS*` or legacy `AAAGAS*` and all 28 settled against `https://gasprices.aaa.com/`.
  - **Compounding impact:** Because both `scripts/fetch-snapshot.mjs` (settled events loop, `series.slice(0, 6)`) and `assets/app.js` (`CFG.seriesPerTopic = 6`) take the top 6 series per topic, `KXNGASMAX` at rank #5 consumed a top-6 slot (fetching 0 live markets and 2 empty natural-gas settled events `KXNGASMAX-B-25DEC29` and `KXNGASMAX-25DEC29` with 0 markets) and displaced genuine AAA retail gasoline series **`KXAAAGASMINTX`** (rank #7, volume 34,785, with active open markets verified live today) from the top-6 settled-event and live-browser capture.
- **Fix:**
  - Added `isGasPriceSeries(s)` in `scripts/fetch-snapshot.mjs` and matching `isGasPriceSeries(s)` in `assets/app.js`, keeping `KXAAAGAS*` / `AAAGAS*` and retail `"gas price"` titles while explicitly rejecting `^(?:KX)?NGAS`, `\bnatural\s+gas\b`, and `diesel` (consistent with `F-05`, `F-18`, and `assets/catalyst.js`).
  - Updated the Gas Prices selection-rule copy in `scripts/fetch-snapshot.mjs`, `assets/app.js`, and `templates/questions.tpl` to match the exact filter.
  - The committed `data/snapshot.json` is automatically refreshed from the live official Demo API by `.github/workflows/refresh-data.yml` when `scripts/fetch-snapshot.mjs` lands on `main`.

### `F-RESOLVED-01` — Four still-active, unresolved contracts rendered inside `categories/gas.html`'s "Resolved events (yes / no)" table
- **Measured on published `categories/gas.html` (`main` @ `44a47d0`/`8838a4a`):**
  - `KXAAAGASMINCA-26DEC31-4.10`, `-4.00`, `-3.90`, and `-3.80` (`status: "active"`, `result: null`, closing `2026-12-31T14:55:00Z`) appeared in BOTH the **Open markets** table (`categories/gas.html` lines 744–874) AND the **Resolved events (yes / no)** table (`categories/gas.html` lines 2048–2079) with blank `Result: —`, `Settlement value: —`, and `Settled at: —`.
  - **Root cause:** `GET /events?status=settled&with_nested_markets=true` returns partially-settled events with every nested market. Neither `scripts/fetch-snapshot.mjs`, `scripts/build-site.mjs` (`resolvedTable()`), nor `assets/app.js` (`pages.category` `#resolved-body`) filtered nested markets for an actual resolved outcome (`yes`, `no`, or `scalar`).
- **Fix:**
  - Added `isResolvedMarket(m)` (`result` is `yes`, `no`, or `scalar`) in `scripts/fetch-snapshot.mjs` (filtering before `.slice(0, 12)` and skipping settled events with 0 resolved markets), `scripts/build-site.mjs` (`resolvedTable()`, plus `neutral` badge class for `scalar`), and `assets/app.js` (`pages.category` `#resolved-body` and `pages.markets` resolved filter).
  - Updated `scripts/check-settlement-ts.mjs` source regex so it preserves its `settlement_ts` assertion with the pre-filtered `resolvedMarkets` variable.
  - **Pre-rebuild negative control proof:** Running `node scripts/check-topic-and-resolved-integrity.mjs` before regenerating `categories/gas.html` failed immediately on the committed site (`gas.html resolved row has unresolved Result cell: Below $4.10 / KXAAAGASMINCA-26DEC31-4.10 -> —`, exit 1); after rebuilding with `node scripts/build-site.mjs`, all 4 active rows are removed from `#resolved-body` while finalized `KXAAAGASMINCA-26DEC31-4.20` (`result: "yes"`, `settlement_ts: 2026-01-22 02:49:56 UTC`) remains intact.

### `F-VOL-01` — Landing page "Topic summary" "24h volume ($)" silently summed only the top-60 stored rows
- **Measured on published `index.html` & `executive-summary.html` (`main` @ `44a47d0`/`8838a4a`):**
  - `scripts/fetch-snapshot.mjs` stored `live_markets_total` across all open markets (`gas: 142`, `elections: 356`, `culture: 0`, `crypto: 1,753`, `golf: 226`) but sliced `live_markets` to `MAX_TOPIC_MARKET_ROWS = 60` without recording the full-topic 24h notional total.
  - `scripts/build-site.mjs` (`topicOverviewTable()`) summed only the 60 stored rows (gas `13,662`, elections `3,411,835`, crypto `373,475`, golf `927,246`) and printed that partial sum beside `1,753` open markets under the unqualified header `24h volume ($)`.
- **Fix:**
  - `scripts/fetch-snapshot.mjs` now computes and stores `live_volume_24h_notional_total` via `sumVolume24hNotional(live)` across all open markets in the topic BEFORE slicing `live_markets` to 60.
  - `scripts/build-site.mjs` (`topicOverviewTable()`) displays `live_volume_24h_notional_total` when present; when reading an older snapshot that omits it and `totalOpen > live.length`, it explicitly labels the cell `top 60 of 1,753` (etc.) so partial scope is never silently misrepresented.
  - `templates/index.tpl` adds a concise sub-note defining `24h volume ($)` as 24-hour contracts × `notional_value_dollars` and distinguishing it from the alert engine's lifetime traded-premium estimate.
