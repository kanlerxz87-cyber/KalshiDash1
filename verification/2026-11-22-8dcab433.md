## 4. Measured cadence — independent corroboration of the published F-83

F-83 is **not this session's row**; it was published by session-b8913775 in PR #96 and reports six
`schedule` runs in 34.6 h (≈1 per 5.8 h). This session measured a narrower window independently, to
test whether that picture held.

Method: `GET /repos/{owner}/{repo}/actions/workflows/<wf>/runs` for `live-alerts.yml` and
`keepalive.yml`, the crons read from those files, `data/live/feed.json` → `meta.generated_at`, and the
sandbox clock (`2026-11-22T08:07:28Z`).

| Workflow | Declared | Observed | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| `live-alerts.yml` | `cron: '*/10 * * * *'` | one `schedule` run at **02:27:01Z**; previous run was a `push` at 23:43:52Z | ≈8.4 h with one scheduled run where the cron implies ~50 |
| `keepalive.yml` | `cron: '4,19,34,49 * * * *'` | 01:36:03Z, 21:34:55Z, 16:02:13Z | ≈4 h and ≈5.5 h between runs where the cron implies 15 min |
| Committed feed | — | `generated_at 2026-11-22T02:27:14.000Z` | ≈5.7 h old at 08:07Z |

The two measurements agree in direction and order of magnitude, so F-83 stands. The **cause** is not
claimed: GitHub does not expose a per-run "dropped" flag, so delay-vs-drop is unverified here.

What stays current regardless: while an alerts page is open, `alerts/js/alerts-page.js` calls
`A.pollLive({maxSeries:8})` every **30 s** (lines 621–622) and re-reads the committed feed every
**60 s** (line 636), re-rendering both chats. Every header prints the measured age of the newest sample
via `assets/freshness.js` (warn at 20 min, stale at 45 min). What does not advance without a scheduled
run: committed alert history, resolutions/awaiting, and the static snapshot fallback.

**Copy correction, superseded rather than duplicated.** This session had qualified the unqualified

34.6 h measurement plus the `keepalive` and `demo-account` crons), so this session's wording was dropped
