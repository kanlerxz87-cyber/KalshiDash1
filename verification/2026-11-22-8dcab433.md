| `keepalive.yml` | `cron: '4,19,34,49 * * * *'` | 01:36:03Z, 21:34:55Z, 16:02:13Z | ≈4 h and ≈5.5 h between runs where the cron implies 15 min |
| Committed feed | — | `generated_at 2026-11-22T02:27:14.000Z` | ≈5.7 h old at 08:07Z |

What is *not* claimed: the cause. The run history is consistent with the documented
delay/drop behaviour, but GitHub does not expose a per-run "dropped" flag, so the cause is
**unverified** and the row says so. What *is* claimed is bounded and reproducible from the links in
the row: the timestamps, the crons, and the feed age.

What stays current regardless: while an alerts page is open, `alerts/js/alerts-page.js` calls
`A.pollLive({maxSeries:8})` every **30 s** (`alerts/js/alerts-page.js:621-622`) and re-reads the committed feed every **60 s**
(`alerts/js/alerts-page.js:636`), re-rendering both chats. Every page header prints the measured age of the newest sample
via `assets/freshness.js` (warn at 20 min, stale at 45 min). What does not advance without a
scheduled run: committed alert history, resolutions/awaiting, and the static snapshot fallback.

**Docs corrected where they over-promised.** `README.md` said the workflow "runs the engine every
10 minutes" without qualification; it now says the cron is *declared*, links the GitHub limitation,
cites today's measurement and F-83, and states that the header prints the measured age.
