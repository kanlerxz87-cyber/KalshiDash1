## 9. F-83 — the dashboard's replayed alerts (found after J2, corrected)

**How it was found:** reading the *live* `dashboard.html` through the page fetcher showed a chat pane
repeating "Will Jon Stewart be the nominee…" with alternating `▲ +0.82 $0.0030 → $0.8220` and
`▼ −0.82 $0.8220 → $0.0030` messages, one per minute. Cross-checking the committed files:

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| `data/live/feed.json` alerts for `KXPRESNOMD-28-JSTE` | **0** — the engine never alerted on this market |
| the market's own captured row | `last_price_dollars 0.0030`, book `$0.002/$0.003` |
| its captured 1-minute closes | alternate `$0.003/$0.004` ↔ `$0.822/$0.823`; **130 adjacent flips ≥ $0.10** in 359 minutes |
| the code that produces them | `assets/app.js` → `seedAlertsFromSnapshot`, comparing adjacent closes |
| why the capture is like this | `scripts/fetch-snapshot.mjs` stores `[end_period_ts, close]` only and discards the OHLC that [the official candlestick schema](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-market-candlesticks) defines (`yes_bid`/`yes_ask`/`price` as open-high-low-close distributions) |
| the delivered test | `check-market-chart.mjs` replayed a 4-candle synthetic fixture, so a contradictory series was never exercised |

**The correction, and why this rule.** The obvious candidates were rejected on evidence: a
"price never leaves a ±$0.05 band around the captured last price" rule throws away every real
≥ $0.10 move (proved against the existing `FIXTURE` case: closes 0.20→0.30 around a captured last of
0.30). The rule adopted is that a replayed move must be **sustained**: every close available in the
next 3 minutes must still be on the new side. That is the minimum evidence a trader could have acted
on it, it is well-defined on closes alone, and it fails exactly where the data is untrustworthy:

| Real capture, the F-83 rule applied | Published | Suppressed & counted |
|---|---|---|
| `KXPRESNOMD-28-JSTE` | **23** | **107** (was 130 published, 0 counted) |
| the other 7 captured series | 0 | 0 (no qualifying move either way) |

**Regression test** (`scripts/check-market-chart.mjs`, 6 new assertions, 37 total) pins all of it and
was verified to **fail on the pre-fix code**: `alternating series published 11 invented swings`.
It also asserts that a sustained move is still published in both directions, that suppressed legs are
counted rather than dropped, and that the replay badge states the close-only evidence limit.

**Deliberately not "fixed" here:** the last close of a series has no later close to contradict it, so
at most one undisprovable leg can survive on a pathological series — the test asserts the collapse,
not a fabricated zero. The real fix is to stop discarding OHLC in `scripts/fetch-snapshot.mjs`
(recorded on F-83 and left for its owner), and the same seeded replay in the preserved archive
(`archive/legacy-root/assets/app.js`) is left untouched.
