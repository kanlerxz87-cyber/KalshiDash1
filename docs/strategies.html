Trade analysis & strategies

Trades are categorized by category (gas / elections / culture / crypto / golf), side (buy/sell), and price bucket (<20¢, 20–40¢, 40–60¢, 60–80¢, >80¢). Category stats from your simulator trades are on the Portfolio page. This page adds backtests: simple rules evaluated on real Kalshi candlesticks for the market selected on the dashboard.

Educational only — not financial advice. Backtests use past prices, ignore fees/slippage unless stated, and do not predict future outcomes. Do not trade real money based on these illustrations.

How to turn results into a strategy

  1. Run each template on several markets per category; compare win rate and P&L per contract.
  2. Check the price-bucket breakdown of your own trades (Portfolio page) — are you profitable in the buckets you actually trade?
  3. Only consider a strategy “promising” if it survives across categories and time windows. Anything else is noise.
  4. Record the chosen path on the Suggestions page before implementing changes.
