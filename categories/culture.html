What a trader should do on this page

  1. Open Dashboard and watch the two chat panes for moves of at least $0.10.
  2. Pick the market, open its detail page and read the chart, the order book and the settlement rules.
  3. Check the ROI score and the liquidity before acting — the model is documented on Strategies.
  4. Record the trade on the Portfolio page; the auto-trader will also run the published strategies for you.
