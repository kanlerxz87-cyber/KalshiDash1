Automated Simulated Trading

Simulated only. This engine executes simulated trades against live public demo prices — no real orders are placed. Real execution requires the account holder's demo API keys and signed requests (POST /portfolio/events/orders — Create Order (V2)), which this static site never requests or stores (flagged F-02). Every simulated trade is recorded with its strategy and reason, then tracked on Portfolio and analyzed on Trade Analysis.

How the engine decides

  1. Watchlist — the same top-dollar live markets watched by the alert feeds (top by 24h dollar volume across the five focus topics).
  2. Gate — only markets the transparent high-ROI model scores at or above the configured minimum are traded (score = 40% liquidity + 30% open interest + 30% opportunity; see Alerts).
  3. Entry — each strategy has a deterministic entry rule (below); positions are simulated at the live last price and tagged with the strategy and the reason.
  4. Exit — open positions are settled when their market resolves yes/no (winning contracts pay $1 — Market Settlement). Positions can also be closed early at the current price if the market price reaches the opposing extreme.
  5. Tracking — every simulated trade flows to Portfolio (P&L, win rate, equity curve) and Trade Analysis (topic × strategy performance).
