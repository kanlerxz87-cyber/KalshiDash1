4. Dashboard capabilities

Live alerting

Two scrolling chats watch the top-dollar markets for contract-price swings ≥ $0.10 either direction using verified trade-by-trade prices: an all-events feed and a high-ROI model filter (transparent 40/30/30 liquidity/open-interest/opportunity heuristic — F-09). Each alert links its API source and the settlement-source websites that may have caused the jump. See Live Alerts.

Tracking, trading, analysis

Markets are tracked until resolution (yes/no). An automated simulated-trade engine tests five strategies against live prices; trades are recorded, settled at resolution, and analyzed by topic and strategy with P&L, win rate and an equity curve. See Automated Trading, Portfolio and Trade Analysis.

Charts and liquidity

Live interactive price graph: X = time (5-minute seconds-granularity samples; 1h/6h/24h minute candles; 7d hourly candles), Y = current contract price. The orderbook ladder shows contracts available at each price (bids straight from the API, asks implied from NO bids). See Market Detail.

Verification discipline

Every table row links the official endpoint or documentation page it came from. Unconfirmed values are left blank and flagged. Sources lists every capture; Flagged for Review publishes all irregularities.

5. Key findings

  1. Liquidity is concentrated in a few markets. At the latest capture the elections market KXELECTIONEMERGENCY-27JAN01 led the focus topics with 7,475.30 contracts traded and equal open interest, followed by the demo hourly BTC market with 2,820.00 total volume (crypto series volume leader KXLTCMAXY: 71,118.00). Gas, golf and culture had no open markets at capture — their series trade on daily/tournament schedules.
  2. Swings happen fast on the hourly demo BTC markets — verified trades show ≥ $0.30 price moves within minutes, so alert polling at 10-second cadence is the best public-data approximation (F-08).
  3. No server-side volume ranking exists (Get Markets sorts by creation time), so "highest dollar" rankings are computed client-side from verified series/market rows (F-03).
  4. Real account sync and real order execution remain unverified — they require the account holder's demo API keys, which this session must never request or store (F-02).
