Live Price Alerts

The dashboard watches the highest-dollar live markets across the five focus topics and posts to two scrolling chats whenever a contract price swings ≥ $0.10 in either direction. The left chat shows all events; the right chat is the selective filter — only markets our high-ROI heuristic model likes (score ≥ 0.50, formula documented below). Every entry links the exact API source that produced the swing, and known settlement-source links are attached as possible catalysts. Swing detection is verified against the public Get Trades endpoint (trade-by-trade prices); when the live API is unreachable, verified snapshot trades are replayed through the same detector.

How the model filter works (transparent, source-linked)

The high-ROI filter is a transparent heuristic computed from verified market fields — it is not an official Kalshi feature and is flagged as such (F-09). For each watched market:

  • liquidity (40%): 24h volume, normalized across the watchlist — deep books mean you can enter/exit without moving the price (Get Market).
  • open interest (30%): contracts already outstanding, normalized across the watchlist — active interest means the event is being traded (Get Market).
  • opportunity (30%): |0.5 − last price| × 2 — a price near the middle has the most room to move either way before resolution (Market Settlement).

ROI proxy: (1 − price) for a YES buy, price for a NO buy, scaled by liquidity. Markets scoring ≥ 0.50 appear in the filtered chat. Catelyst links shown on alerts come from each series' verified settlement_sources (AAA gas prices, EIA, CF Benchmarks, official election authorities, PGA/tour sources, etc.) — these are the events/websites that may have caused a sharp jump; the site never claims a causal link it cannot verify.
