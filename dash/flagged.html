The official parameter list for Get Events has no category filter, and the event category field is marked deprecated. F-08 Alert detection depends on REST polling — fast swings may be missed The demo API has no server-side swing alerting, and the official WebSocket requires an authenticated session even for public channels (this site never handles credentials — F-02), so alerts run on REST: a 15 s batched quote poll (GET /markets?tickers=…) plus a trade-by-trade walk (GET /markets/trades) of the 4 highest-dollar tracked markets, each trade processed exactly once. Swings shorter than the cycle, or occurring between cycles, may be missed or detected with delayed magnitude. REST responses are cached and can lag the exchange by several seconds. Get Trades docs · Get Markets (caching caveat) · WebSocket quick start (session auth) — (alerts only fire on verified ≥ $0.10 price differences actually observed; no sub-second latency is claimed anywhere). Confirm whether a sub-15s poll or a server-side scanner (S-03/S-09) is acceptable for stricter latency; with demo API keys a WebSocket client can replace the poller.
F-09 The "high-ROI" chat filter is a transparent heuristic, not an official Kalshi model Two deterministic, published scores filter and rank: (1) four-component score 0.35·liquidity + 0.20·spread + 0.25·momentum + 0.20·band (gates the selective chat at ≥ 70 and the automated momentum-10c/fade-jump strategies); (2) normalized ROI proxy 0.40·liquidity + 0.30·open interest + 0.30·opportunity (shown on every alert, used by the Automated Trading engine). No Kalshi model or ROI data exists in the public API, so the filter is the site's own documented heuristic. Get Market schema · Market Settlement The scores and their inputs are shown on every alert and in the ranking table; "model" is the brief's terminology, with both formulas published on the Alerts page. Review the weightings; adjust if desired (single constants block in dash/js/alerts.js).
F-10 Automated simulated trading concentrates on the hourly demo BTC markets The most active focus-topic markets are the demo hourly BTC series (KXDEMOFUTHOURLY-*) — they settle hourly and carry most of the observed volume. Automated strategies therefore exercise simulated positions mostly on these markets; gas/golf/culture/elections markets are thin or absent at capture. live market query · demo_env (mock prices) No inference about real-market behavior is made; all P&L from these simulated trades is labeled simulated. Confirm the strategy set should keep trading the thinner topics or restrict to the most liquid markets.

Blank-value policy

When a value could not be confirmed from an official source it is displayed as empty/“—” and listed above. No number on this site was copied from memory, guessed, or interpolated. Prices shown to 4 decimals and counts to 2 decimals follow the fixed-point conventions in Fixed-Point Representation.
