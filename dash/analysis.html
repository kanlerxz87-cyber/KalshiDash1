Findings from the verified data

  1. Liquidity is concentrated in few markets. At the verified snapshot (2026-11-20 11:10 UTC) one elections market (KXELECTIONEMERGENCY-27JAN01, 7,475.30 contracts) and one crypto market (KXDEMOFUTHOURLY-26NOV20H12-UP, 1,175.00) dominated the focus topics; the rest were empty or thin. Thin markets make entry/exit cost dominate — check the orderbook ladder before sizing any trade. (row sources)
  2. Topic schedules differ sharply. Crypto demo markets trade hourly; gas prices resolve daily/monthly against AAA/EIA publications; golf around tournament rounds; elections events can run for months. A one-size polling and monitoring cadence over-trades nothing and misses the schedules — the tracker therefore shows close times per row.
  3. Resolution economics are simple; price limits are not. Each winning contract pays exactly $1, but markets use different tick grids (price_ranges: 1¢, 0.1¢, tapered structures). Limit orders must snap to the grid or the API rejects them — verified in the fixed-point docs.
  4. Demo ≠ production. Kalshi warns demo market prices and behavior may not reflect real markets (demo_env.md). Any strategy proven here needs re-validation on production data before real-money use.
