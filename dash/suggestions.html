Suggestions Board

Every proposed improvement to the dashboard and its decision-making value. Nothing here is implemented until it is reviewed and approved — use the buttons to move items through proposed → approved / rejected / implemented. Decisions are saved in your browser (localStorage). Each suggestion links the verified evidence behind it.

How suggestions are generated

  1. Evidence first: only claims that survived the line-by-line verification (see Sources) may justify a suggestion.
  2. Gap-driven: anything missing to make a better decision becomes a row on Questions & Gaps first, then a suggestion.
  3. Review before build: impact vs effort is stated up front; the reviewer approves, rejects, or edits before any code changes.
  4. No silent changes: implemented items are re-verified and logged in the repo's VERIFICATION.md.
